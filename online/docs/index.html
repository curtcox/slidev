Slidev Online

Edit slides.md and style.css in your browser. The preview updates after a pause. Use Render to retry after an error.

Share creates a link with your Markdown and CSS. QR shows a code for that link. HashBin saving needs a registered client ID.

Links open their own document. To recover earlier work, choose More, then Resume saved work. Recovered drafts and imported files wait for Render.

On phones, switch between Slides, Markdown and CSS. Desktop also offers Split view. Present opens the slide preview in fullscreen when your browser supports it.

Download saves Markdown, CSS or a JSON document with both. ZIP download, the gallery and further presentation tools are still being built.
